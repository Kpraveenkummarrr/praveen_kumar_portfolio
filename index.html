aboutWho I Am

Three years, 38 districts, zero funding.

I'm a backend developer based in Tiruchirappalli, currently building enterprise systems at Ardhas Technology by day — HRMS platforms, incident dashboards, and the RESTful APIs other teams depend on.

On my own time, I've spent close to three years building the TN Smart Bus System — alone, without funding, without a team — because Tamil Nadu commuters deserved something better than scattered PDFs and WhatsApp forwards for bus timings. When the State Express Transport Corporation formally acknowledged the proposal, it was the first real signal that the work mattered past my own GitHub graph.

I'm drawn to problems with real edge cases — concurrent bookings, multilingual queries, dispatch systems with zero room for manual error. The bus project is where I get to choose the edge cases myself.
